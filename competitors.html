Roof Right Roofing Services has a "roofer Minot ND" page — and over 215,000 others on subdomains for towns all over North America, answered by toll-free 888 numbers. That is the shape of a lead-selling network, not a local contractor.

The real competition in Minot is smaller than it looks, and one firm in it is doing this properly.

What Each One Tells You
ABC Seamless of North Dakota — the one doing it properly

A national siding and roofing franchise, trading since 1978, with 447 pages and 167 of them aimed at Minot and North Dakota searches — "energy-efficient windows Minot", "gutter protection Minot", an areas-served Minot page. Hail comes up twelve times on the homepage. Tag Manager is installed, so they can see what works.

This is who you are really competing with for the storm-season search. They have the pages, the measurement and the history. What they do not have is two local owners on the roof.

Roof Right Roofing Services — worth understanding, not copying

Over 215,000 pages on town subdomains across the US and into Quebec, with 888 toll-free numbers. Businesses built this way typically collect the enquiry and pass it on to whichever contractor pays for it. We have not seen how they operate and make no claim about it — but a homeowner who calls them is not calling a Minot firm, and if you have ever paid for "leads", it is worth knowing where those leads may come from.

Anderson Roofing and Leroy's — the local field

Both are genuine Minot businesses with small websites. Anderson has six pages and mentions hail four times; Leroy's has a single-page style site with Google Analytics and no hail content. Neither is out-building you online. The local field is thin, which is good news for a firm that gets its site back and builds it out.

Where You Can Actually Win
What you have that the big players cannot copy
  • Two owners who are on the job. A franchise and a lead network cannot say that. It is the single most persuasive thing about you, and it should be on every page.
  • You already sell storm damage restoration — it is on your own Facebook banner. A set of Minot hail pages, live before the next storm, puts you in the search ABC Seamless currently owns.
  • The local field is thin. Anderson and Leroy's have small sites. Beating them online is a realistic, near-term goal.
  • You have the trust already — BBB accredited and A+, a five-year workmanship warranty, and a Facebook page where every reviewer recommends you.
  • Real job content exists. Crew photos and an active TikTok are exactly what a lead network cannot fake.
What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Your own site. It went offline this morning, so page counts, tracking and content could not be assessed.
  • Search rankings. No rank tracker was run for any firm. Page counts are structural evidence, not a ranking report.
  • Google Business Profiles and review counts. Not compared for any firm, including yours.
  • Anyone's spend, leads or revenue. We have seen public websites only.
  • Other Minot roofers. This covers the firms we could identify and check today. It is not a complete list of every roofer in the area.

One serious competitor, one lead network, and a thin local field.

The market is winnable. It starts with getting your own site back online.
